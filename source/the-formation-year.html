Prologue

Before the first meeting

The space already worked. People showed up, tended it, built things in it, and treated it as theirs. But on paper it belonged to one LLC and one person's signature: the lease, the insurance, the liability, and the gap between what the community contributed and what the building cost, all carried privately. The ask that started everything was practical: form a container that can hold what the community already does, so the legal reality catches up to the lived one.

That is the least glamorous reason a cooperative has ever been formed, and the group knew it. What follows is the story of how a conversation that began with a lease found its way, meeting by meeting, to everything else.

Act I · August to September 2025

The gift and the ground

Four meetings in which the group asked why, found its metaphor, and chose what to do with surplus before it had any.

Meeting 01Aug 8, 2025
The Why and the What

Two practical drivers, and a question of worth

The founding conversation named its reasons plainly: distribute the liability, and align the legal form with a practice that was already cooperative. Three documents were laid out like geological layers, articles slowest, agreements fastest. Then the conversation went somewhere contracts rarely do: how to recognize contribution without commodifying it, whether gratitude itself could be a patronage mechanism, and three nested ways of being in economic relationship: giving together, saving together, investing together.

Carried forwardThe three rings still order our relationship to capital. Gratitude without a number is now proposed at the schema level.

Meeting 02Sep 5, 2025
Visioning the Scenius

The word arrives

A round-robin visioning: what this place is, what it could become, what each person hoped to be within it. The answers ranged from buying the building to seeding an alternative economy, but the lasting gift was vocabulary. Scenius, the collective form of genius. Third space. Co-incubator. And a first structural instinct: the February gathering then on the horizon would live inside the cooperative as a project, not beside it as a stranger.

Carried forwardScenius became the public benefit purpose named in the filed articles.

Meeting 03Sep 12, 2025
Synthesis and Soil

Soil, not firm

Two purpose drafts lay on the table: one energizing but vague, one submittable but voiceless. The group chose to weave them. Then came the meeting's defining move: RegenHub is substrate, not firm. Coworking revenue is maintenance, like network fees; the living value grows from the ground, in ventures the cooperative holds relationships to rather than routing through itself. In one stroke this dissolved the fear of metering every hour, and Goodhart's law was named the standing design constraint.

Carried forwardSoil, not firm, still governs the accounting, and Goodhart still guards every metric.

Meeting 04Sep 19, 2025
Community Reinvestment

What to do with surplus, decided while broke

The facilitator was away; another member led, and the group made one of its clearest choices. Offered dividend checks or grants to community projects, it chose grants: revenue paves the roads. Membership was settled as a matter of orientation over output, the growth-minded student as welcome as the veteran builder. And the group told itself the truth about price: a real third space costs little or nothing to be in, which this one did not, yet.

Carried forwardReinvestment over distribution is now the patronage plan's spine; the third place stays honestly labeled an aspiration.

The group decided what to do with a surplus it did not yet have. That may be the most cooperative act in the whole archive.

Act II · September to October 2025

Purpose and protocols

Three meetings in which the theory got wilder, the structure got simpler, and the first neighbor projects came to show what the soil could grow.

Meeting 05Sep 26, 2025
Post-Capitalist Protocols

The widest aperture

With the purpose edits accepted, the group let itself think past capitalism for an afternoon: mutual staking, credit anyone can issue, money decoupled from worth, the garden extending the soil. Three capital pools were sketched, giving, saving, and investing, echoing the first meeting's rings. Not all of it would survive contact with a filing deadline. All of it shaped what the cooperative refuses to become.

Carried forwardThe three pools are now a proposed treasury dimension, so what funded what stays a query, not a mystery.

Meeting 06Sep 26, 2025
Implementation Afternoon

Simplified by evening

The same day, an evening session traded theory for shipping. Membership collapsed from a matrix to three honest tiers: community participants, coworking members, cooperative members with one vote each. The website was updated before the night ended. Someone traced genius to its origin, a household guardian spirit rather than a lone talent, and the room heard scenius as a return, not an invention. The lawyer was contacted.

Carried forwardThe three tiers still stand, and the commons now records them as agreements with versions and standing.

Meeting 07Oct 3, 2025
Revenets and Revenue Sharing

Trust before tools

The first project presentation: a neighborhood-building program spanning a hundred-plus stewards from Boulder to Perth, carrying a principle the cooperative adopted on contact: technology must follow relationship, never lead it. A Baltimore housing cooperative showed what worker ownership and ecological credit could look like grown up. Server hardware was offered toward shared computing. The soil had its first visiting gardeners.

Carried forwardTrust before tools became the quiet law of the commons build: the record serves the relationships, not the reverse.

Act III · October to December 2025

Counsel and the split

Four meetings in which an outside practice held up a mirror, a lawyer posed the hard question, and the group answered it by becoming two things instead of one.

Meeting 08Oct 10, 2025
Projects and Place

Admin as power center

A member's design practice showed the group its own future: clients invited into the live creative process, a single source of truth where change propagates coherently, and a blunt observation that every organization holds three irreducible administrative functions, accounting, finance, and legal, which in ordinary firms operate as opaque centers of control. The implication for a cooperative was left standing in the room: transparency there is where values are embodied or undermined.

Carried forwardThe Common Information System is that observation, built: the administrative center made into glass.

Meeting 09Nov 21, 2025
Legal Consultation

The mirror of counsel

Jeff Pote of Pote Law joined and asked the questions that form entities. Multi-stakeholder boards can fracture between classes. Provisional membership can let both sides evaluate before co-ownership. Public benefit costs nothing but a commitment in the articles and an honest annual report, enforceable only by members. And beneath it all, the taxation fork: Subchapter T's patronage flexibility versus Subchapter K's freedom from employment law for a community whose work is mostly voluntary. The group named a new species for itself in the discussion after: a venture commons.

Carried forwardThe annual narrative report is becoming a projection of the record itself, every figure traceable.

Meeting 10Nov 28, 2025
Partnership vs. Corporation

Two entities, one intention

The fork resolved into a dual strategy, worked out in real time. First: a partnership of real persons under Subchapter K, equal shares, voluntary contribution, no payroll machinery, holding the space and the lease. Later, when ventures justify it: a cooperative of organizations under Subchapter T, unlocking capital accounts and patronage allocation, the venture studio. Build the ventures first; let the structure follow the life, not precede it.

Carried forwardThe partnership is filed and real. The studio remains honestly labeled: anticipated, and waiting on the harvest.

Meeting 11Dec 12, 2025
Formation and Strategy

From deliberation to deadline

Execution arrived: file by end of January, present the cooperative at the February gathering. The minimum viable filing turned out to be three pages. A scheduling app being built in the space became the first case study of a venture emerging from the soil, its tokenized revenue shares a seed for the future studio. And in the condensed purpose statement, one phrase stopped the room: the collective intelligence that emerges when culturally aligned people share sustained proximity.

Carried forwardThat sentence is in the filed articles. It is the reason this room exists tonight.

Act IV · January to February 2026

Two languages and the vessel

Four meetings in which the cooperative learned to speak twice, held its nerve through the crunch, and was born on a Friday.

Meeting 12Jan 16, 2026
Expansion, Grants, Education

Fluent in both

A framing arrived that the cooperative still lives by: speak the language of capitalism, desks, event space, infrastructure, and the language of public benefit, stewardship, regenerative public goods, and let everyone associated with the place be fluent in both. The third floor of the building surfaced as an opportunity; a learning hub took shape as its leading idea; a community-hub grant was identified and claimed as a task. The revenue landscape, for the first time, looked like a landscape.

Carried forwardTwo languages, one record: the same figures now read honestly in either tongue.

Meeting 13Jan 23, 2026
Finalization

Twenty-three minutes in the crunch

The shortest meeting in the archive, held in the thick of event preparation. The retainer needed one more signature cycle. The purpose statement stood at its fourth version and was called ready. A trademark search was the last manual step. Identity and brand work was consciously deferred: the community's bandwidth was fully spoken for, and the group said so instead of pretending otherwise.

Carried forwardNaming capacity honestly, including its limits, became a habit the status marks now enforce.

Meeting 14Jan 30, 2026
Status, Data, Third Floor

An information right

With filing days away, the conversation reached a century out. Contributed servers were being readied for local hosting; the community data trust idea matured into an argument: alongside the housing right there is an information right, and the first century of computing was shaped by the corporate co-option of tools first imagined for collective coordination. The building, committed a hundred years as information infrastructure, was spoken aloud as a destination. The third floor's structure clarified: a separate vessel, with the cooperative holding an interest.

Carried forwardThe walkaway rehearsal runs weekly: the community's data can stand up on fresh ground, byte for byte, any week it must.

Meeting 15Feb 6, 2026
The Birth of the Vessel

Filed

On February 6, 2026, the articles of organization were filed with the State of Colorado, and RegenHub became a Limited Cooperative Association and Public Benefit entity, six months to the season after the first meeting. The facilitator's role was recognized as Ventures and Operations Steward. Techne stepped forward as the first named initiative, carrying the old Greek unity of art and craft and a position on the biggest question in the room: AI beside labor augments; AI on capital's side of the ledger replaces. And the soil metaphor returned home, in the form of a question put to the first fruiting program: what do you intend to cultivate here, what do you want to retain, and what reciprocity do you imagine flowing back?

Carried forwardThat question is now the cooperative's standing question, asked of every venture the soil grows.

Epilogue · February to August 2026

The year the record grew

Formation ended and tending began. The bank account opened, the insurance bound, the bylaws and membership agreement went through their cycles toward ratification. Techne widened from a co-op of many crafts toward a co-op of practice, the art, the craft, and the science held in one old word. And the observation from Meeting 8, that the administrative center is where values are embodied or undermined, became a build: the Commonplace Book, the cooperative's shared record, where every agreement carries its version, every figure traces to its events, and every rule cites the clause that justifies it.

By midsummer, a member could join, sign, and appear in the directory without a meeting; see every gathering and be counted where the record counts; post an offer to play, practice, or work, and watch what it led to. The rest is honestly marked: some of it drafted, some anticipated, some open, none of it pretending. The dialogue you have just scrolled through did not end. It changed medium.

August 14, 2026

One year, almost to the week, from the first meeting. The cooperative gathers to ratify what the dialogue built and to open the book to its members. If you are reading this in the room: you are standing in the soil this story described, and the next chapter of the record has a place in it for what you carry.
