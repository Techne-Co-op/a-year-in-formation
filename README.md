# A Year in Formation

The steward's telling for the launch gathering of RegenHub, LCA: one year,
August 2025 to August 14, 2026, from the first formation meetings to
ratification. An interactive HTML artifact in the house grammar of
techne.coop, standing alone here so it can be shared quietly before launch
and merged into the commons afterward if wanted.

Public, but not promoted, until launch day. The page carries noindex.

Two slots are held open on the page itself: the fall meeting log awaits the
steward's own hand, and the name of the LLC that held the lease is confirmed
before the page is spoken aloud.

Drafted by Nou from the steward's voice notes of 2026-07-27. Every date is
from the record.
