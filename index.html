i · the honest beginning

We organized around the least exciting thing we had

Kevin held the lease and the insurance for the third floor of 1515 Walnut in an LLC of his own, and asked for a container that could hold them on its own feet: a self-sustaining entity, dependent on no one person's balance sheet.

A cooperative formed around rent can honestly say what it is; one formed around a vision statement has to prove it. We started from the ground and set out to earn the vision.

iii · the role that emerged

Kevin asked me about my ikigai

A pattern for asking what your felt purpose is leading you toward, and how to apply your full self to it. Kevin's question let me craft the steward's role at the intersection of my own ground: an information technologist who found it hard to articulate what he does, why it matters, and who it serves.

The system I could never quite describe about my own work is the one I built so the cooperative would never have that problem.the steward, first partially compensated member of the cooperative

v · two initiatives, one composition

An orchestra, not a soloist

Program 01 · RegenHub

The container for the co-working space: the third floor, the lease, the daily practice of a shared physical place.

Program 02 · Techne

Placemaking intentionality carried into virtual space. A co-op of many crafts: the art, craft, and science of practice.

Fields coordinated with one another compound the way an orchestra compounds: no instrument group sounds complete alone. The Commons exists so composition can be seen, valued, and shared honestly.
