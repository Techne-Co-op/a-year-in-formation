i · the honest beginning

We organized around the least exciting thing we had

The space already worked. People showed up, tended it, and treated it as theirs. But on paper it belonged to one LLC and one person's signature: the lease, the insurance, the liability, all carried privately. The ask that started everything was practical: form a container that can hold what the community already does, so the legal reality catches up to the lived one.

That is the least glamorous reason a cooperative has ever been formed, and the group knew it. A cooperative formed around rent can honestly say what it is; one formed around a vision statement has to prove it. We started from the ground and set out to earn the vision.

act i · august to september 2025

The gift and the ground

Four meetings in which the group asked why, found its metaphor, and chose what to do with surplus before it had any.

01 · Aug 8 · the why and the what

Two practical drivers, and a question of worth

Distribute the liability; align the legal form with a practice already cooperative. Then: can gratitude itself be patronage? Three rings of economic relationship: giving, saving, investing together.

carriedThe three rings still order our relationship to capital.

02 · Sep 5 · visioning the scenius

The word arrives

A round-robin visioning, from buying the building to seeding an alternative economy. The lasting gift was vocabulary: scenius, the collective form of genius.

carriedScenius became the public benefit purpose in the filed articles.

03 · Sep 12 · synthesis and soil

Soil, not firm

RegenHub is substrate: coworking revenue is maintenance, and the living value grows in ventures the cooperative holds relationships to. Goodhart's law named the standing design constraint.

carriedSoil, not firm, still governs the accounting.

04 · Sep 19 · community reinvestment

What to do with surplus, decided while broke

Offered dividend checks or grants to community projects, the group chose grants: revenue paves the roads. Membership settled as orientation over output.

carriedReinvestment over distribution is the patronage plan's spine.

The group decided what to do with a surplus it did not yet have. That may be the most cooperative act in the whole archive.the steward's note

act ii · september to october 2025

Purpose and protocols

Three meetings in which the theory got wilder, the structure got simpler, and the first neighbor projects came to show what the soil could grow.

05 · Sep 26 · post-capitalist protocols

The widest aperture

An afternoon past capitalism: mutual staking, credit anyone can issue, three capital pools echoing the first meeting's rings. Not all of it survived the filing deadline; all of it shaped what the cooperative refuses to become.

carriedThe three pools are a proposed treasury dimension: what funded what stays a query, not a mystery.

06 · Sep 26 · implementation afternoon

Simplified by evening

The same day, theory traded for shipping: membership collapsed to three honest tiers, the website updated before the night ended. Genius traced to its origin, a household guardian spirit; scenius heard as a return, not an invention.

carriedThe three tiers still stand, recorded now as agreements with versions and standing.

07 · Oct 3 · revenets and revenue sharing

Trust before tools

The first visiting gardeners: a neighborhood-building program across a hundred-plus stewards, carrying a principle adopted on contact: technology must follow relationship, never lead it.

carriedTrust before tools became the quiet law of the commons build.

act iii · october to december 2025

Counsel and the split

Four meetings in which an outside practice held up a mirror, a lawyer posed the hard question, and the group answered by becoming two things instead of one.

08 · Oct 10 · projects and place

Admin as power center

Every organization holds three irreducible administrative functions: accounting, finance, legal, ordinarily opaque centers of control. For a cooperative, transparency there is where values are embodied or undermined.

carriedThe Common Information System is that observation, built: the administrative center made into glass.

09 · Nov 21 · legal consultation

The mirror of counsel

Jeff Pote asked the questions that form entities: board fracture, provisional membership, the taxation fork. The group named a new species for itself: a venture commons.

carriedThe annual report is becoming a projection of the record itself.

10 · Nov 28 · partnership vs corporation

Two entities, one intention

The fork resolved: a partnership of real persons holding the space and the lease now; a cooperative of organizations, the venture studio, later, when the ventures justify it. Let the structure follow the life.

carriedThe partnership is filed and real; the studio stays honestly marked anticipated.

11 · Dec 12 · formation and strategy

From deliberation to deadline

File by end of January; present at the February gathering. And one phrase stopped the room: the collective intelligence that emerges when culturally aligned people share sustained proximity.

carriedThat sentence is in the filed articles. It is why this room exists tonight.

act iv · january to february 2026

Two languages and the vessel

Four meetings in which the cooperative learned to speak twice, held its nerve through the crunch, and was born on a Friday.

12 · Jan 16 · expansion, grants, education

Fluent in both

Speak the language of capitalism, desks and infrastructure, and the language of public benefit, stewardship and regenerative public goods, and let everyone here be fluent in both.

carriedTwo languages, one record: the same figures read honestly in either tongue.

13 · Jan 23 · finalization

Twenty-three minutes in the crunch

The shortest meeting in the archive, held in the thick of event preparation. Brand work consciously deferred: the community's bandwidth was fully spoken for, and the group said so instead of pretending otherwise.

carriedNaming capacity honestly became a habit the status marks now enforce.

14 · Jan 30 · status, data, third floor

An information right

Days from filing, the conversation reached a century out: alongside the housing right, an information right; the building spoken aloud as information infrastructure committed a hundred years.

carriedThe walkaway rehearsal runs weekly: the record can stand up on fresh ground, byte for byte.

15 · Feb 6 · the birth of the vessel

Filed

The articles filed with Colorado; RegenHub became an LCA and public benefit entity, six months to the season after the first meeting. The facilitator recognized as Ventures and Operations Steward; Techne stepped forward as the first named initiative.

carriedIts question is now the standing question: what do you intend to cultivate here, and what reciprocity flows back?

interlude · the role that emerged

Kevin asked me about my ikigai

A pattern for asking what your felt purpose is leading you toward, and how to apply your full self to it. Kevin's question let me craft the role Meeting 15 would recognize as Ventures and Operations Steward, at the intersection of my own ground: an information technologist who found it hard to articulate what he does, why it matters, and who it serves.

The system I could never quite describe about my own work is the one I built so the cooperative would never have that problem.the steward, first partially compensated member of the cooperative
